# SKU Performance Transparency Report

## Executive Summary
This report provides a comprehensive analysis of the performance of nine SKUs, highlighting their return on investment (ROI), marketing effectiveness, and labor costs. The total ROI from these SKUs is $10,326.41, with a marketing spend of $23,160.86 and a labor cost of $1,550.42. The analysis reveals significant underperformance across several SKUs, prompting a mix of manual reviews, rejections, and auto-approvals to optimize future strategies.

## Workflow Overview
The SKU performance analysis follows an 8-stage process:

1. **Trigger**: Identification of SKUs underperforming against sales forecasts.
2. **Pricing**: Evaluation of price adjustments to enhance competitiveness.
3. **Marketing**: Assessment of marketing strategies employed for each SKU.
4. **Labor**: Analysis of labor costs associated with SKU management.
5. **Finance**: Calculation of ROI based on revenue generated versus costs incurred.
6. **Product**: Review of product inventory and lead times affecting sales.
7. **Governance**: Decision-making process regarding SKU management (manual review, rejection, or auto-approval).
8. **Report**: Compilation of findings and recommendations for stakeholders.

## Key Findings
- **Top Performer**: SKU_0007 achieved the highest ROI of $3,759 despite being underperforming by 12.9%.
- **Concerns**: Several SKUs (notably SKU_0005 and SKU_0008) showed significant underperformance, with declines exceeding 70% against forecasts.
- **Patterns**: Labor costs exceeding $200 triggered manual reviews for multiple SKUs, indicating a need for cost management strategies.

## Decision Analysis
- **Manual Reviews**: 5 SKUs required manual review due to various concerns, including high labor costs and inventory issues.
- **Rejections**: 3 SKUs were rejected due to negative ROI, indicating they are not viable for continued investment.
- **Auto-Approvals**: 1 SKU was auto-approved as it met all performance guardrails.

## Financial Impact
- **Total ROI**: $10,326.41
- **Total Marketing Spend**: $23,160.86
- **Total Labor Cost**: $1,550.42
- **Average ROI per SKU**: Approximately $1,148.49, indicating a need for improved performance across the board.

- **Marketing Spend**: $1,899 on display ads
- **Labor Cost**: 14.7 hours ($195)

## Recommendations
1. **Cost Management**: Review labor costs associated with SKUs to ensure they remain within budgetary limits.
2. **Performance Monitoring**: Implement a more robust monitoring system to track SKU performance against forecasts regularly.
3. **Marketing Strategy**: Reassess marketing strategies for underperforming SKUs, focusing on channels that yield better ROI.
4. **Inventory Management**: Address inventory issues for SKUs with long lead times to prevent stockouts and lost sales opportunities.

## Next Steps
- Schedule a meeting with the marketing team to discuss revised strategies for underperforming SKUs.
- Conduct a detailed review of labor costs and identify areas for efficiency improvements.
- Monitor the performance of the auto-approved SKU closely to ensure it meets expectations in the upcoming quarter. 

This report serves as a foundation for strategic decision-making and operational improvements aimed at enhancing SKU performance and maximizing ROI.
