# SKU Performance Transparency Report

## Executive Summary
This report provides a comprehensive analysis of SKU performance across nine products, evaluating their sales against forecasts and the effectiveness of marketing strategies. The total return on investment (ROI) from these SKUs is $10,326.41, with a total marketing spend of $23,160.86 and labor costs of $1,550.42. The analysis reveals significant underperformance in several SKUs, necessitating targeted actions to optimize sales and marketing strategies.

## Workflow Overview
The SKU performance analysis follows an 8-stage process:

1. **Trigger**: Identification of SKUs underperforming against sales forecasts.
2. **Pricing**: Adjustments made to SKU prices to enhance competitiveness.
3. **Marketing**: Allocation of marketing resources across various channels.
4. **Labor**: Assessment of labor costs associated with SKU management.
5. **Finance**: Calculation of ROI based on marketing spend and labor costs.
6. **Product**: Evaluation of product inventory and lead times.
7. **Governance**: Decision-making process regarding SKU management (manual review, auto-approval, rejection).
8. **Report**: Compilation of findings and recommendations for stakeholders.

## Key Findings
- **Top Performer**: SKU_0007 ranked first with an ROI of $3,759, despite being underperforming by 12.9%.
- **Major Concerns**: Several SKUs exhibited significant underperformance, with SKU_0005 and SKU_0008 showing declines of 74.6% and 75.7%, respectively.
- **Patterns**: A majority of underperforming SKUs required manual reviews due to high labor costs or inventory concerns.

## Decision Analysis
- **Manual Reviews**: 5 SKUs required manual review due to various concerns, including high labor costs and inventory issues.
- **Rejections**: 3 SKUs were rejected due to negative ROI, indicating a need for strategic reevaluation.
- **Auto-Approved**: Only 1 SKU was auto-approved, demonstrating that most SKUs require closer scrutiny.

## Financial Impact
- **Total ROI**: $10,326.41
- **Total Marketing Spend**: $23,160.86
- **Total Labor Cost**: $1,550.42
- The overall financial analysis indicates that while some SKUs are performing well, the high marketing spend relative to labor costs raises questions about the efficiency of current strategies.

## Individual SKU Stories

### SKU SKU_0007 (Rank #1)
- **Trigger**: Underperforming by 12.9%
- **Price Change**: Increased from $64.43 to $67.82
- **Marketing Spend**: $1,899 on display ads
- **Labor Cost**: 14.7 hours ($195)
- **ROI**: $3,759
- **Decision**: Manual review due to inventory concerns and long lead time (18 days).

### SKU SKU_0006 (Rank #2)
- **Trigger**: Underperforming by 62.7%
- **Price Change**: Increased from $87.00 to $91.58
- **Marketing Spend**: $786 on Instagram
- **Labor Cost**: 16.5 hours ($52)
- **ROI**: $2,418
- **Decision**: Auto-approved as all guardrails were satisfied.

### SKU SKU_0015 (Rank #3)
- **Trigger**: Underperforming by 39.7%
- **Price Change**: Increased from $81.98 to $86.29
- **Marketing Spend**: $3,924 across Email and TikTok
- **Labor Cost**: 8.5 hours ($248)
- **ROI**: $2,087
- **Decision**: Manual review due to labor costs exceeding the $200 cap.

## Recommendations
1. **Reassess Marketing Strategies**: Focus on high-performing channels and reduce spend on underperforming SKUs.
2. **Inventory Management**: Improve inventory forecasting to align with expected sales lifts, particularly for SKUs requiring manual review.
3. **Cost Control**: Implement stricter controls on labor costs associated with SKU management to avoid exceeding budget caps.

## Next Steps
1. **Conduct Manual Reviews**: Prioritize manual reviews for SKUs with high labor costs and inventory concerns.
2. **Revise Marketing Plans**: Develop targeted marketing strategies for underperforming SKUs based on data-driven insights.
3. **Monitor Performance**: Establish a regular review cycle to assess SKU performance and adjust strategies accordingly.

This report serves as a foundation for strategic decision-making aimed at optimizing SKU performance and enhancing overall business profitability.
